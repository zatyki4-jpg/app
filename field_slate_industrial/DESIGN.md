## Brand & Style
The design system embodies rugged, high-precision industrial utility engineered for unpredictable outdoor job site environments and meticulous back-office fiscal compliance. It bridges the physical reality of steep-slope construction—glare, dust, heavy work gloves, and rapid on-site decisions—with the technical rigor of automated AI roof geometry estimation and strict European e-invoicing mandates (Peppol / KSeF).

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Brutalism** with **High-Contrast Precision Tooling**:
- Utilitarian surfaces built from cold slate, basalt, and oxidized carbon steel.
- High-visibility safety accents borrowed directly from heavy machinery, hazardous warning bands, and protective workwear.
- Strict visual hierarchy that eliminates ambiguous decorative flourishes in favor of reinforced touch targets, crisp structural separation, and instant cognitive parsing under direct sunlight or low-light rain conditions.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for high-glare legibility, field safety compliance, and clear semantic divergence between physical measurements, financial clearances, and algorithmic insights.

### Base Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Deep obsidian slate. Absorbs ambient glare and preserves high-contrast legibility in harsh outdoor sun.
- **Surface Tier 1 / Structural Substrate (`#131A26`):** Deep industrial charcoal for primary containers, card frames, and calculation panels.
- **Surface Tier 2 / Elevated Modules (`#1E2738`):** Chiseled slate container background for interactive input zones, modal sheets, and nested roof segment blocks.
- **Surface Tier 3 / Raised Overlays (`#28354A`):** Active pressed states, focused form containers, and floating inspector modules.

### Semantic & Functional Accents
- **Primary / Industrial Safety Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Actionable triggers, primary operational buttons, safety warnings, and field punch-list priority states.
- **Secondary / Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** AI raster vision overlays, lidar pitch-detection wireframes, CAD vector analysis, and automated roof facet segmentations.
- **Tertiary / Fiscal Compliance Emerald (`#10B981`):** Peppol & KSeF real-time transmission receipts, balanced accounting ledgers, approved structural sign-offs.
- **Alert / Structural Fault Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Shearing risks, load failure zones, invalid tax IDs, critical structural defects, and negative margin warnings.
- **Warning / Orange Flare (`#F97316`):** Secondary callouts, weather risk advisories, and time-sensitive invoice processing holds.

### Text & Outline Tiers
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp pure off-white for metrics, pitch degrees, totals, and active controls.
- **Text Medium-Emphasis (`#94A3B8`):** Muted slate for unit labels (mm, m², PLN, EUR), structural tags, and field metadata.
- **Text Low-Emphasis (`#64748B`):** De-emphasized guide text and structural gridlines.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#334155`):** High-definition structural borders ensuring clear spatial division even on low-tier mobile panels.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy is built on three dedicated typographic pillars, designed for harsh field visibility and absolute financial clarity:

1. **Display & Structural Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers an authoritative, engineered character reminiscent of architectural blueprints and industrial site placards.
2. **Operational Body Copy (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Selected for its high x-height, open apertures, and crisp rendering on OLED panels in outdoor glare.
3. **Measurement, Accounting & Protocol Ledger (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width tabular figures used universally for all millimeter/square-meter dimensions, net/VAT/gross amounts, slope degree calculations, and 32-character Peppol/KSeF transactional hash identification strings. Numbers never shift horizontally during live recalculation.

### Implementation Rules
- Apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` across all numerical fields to ensure vertical decimal alignment on multi-line material bids.
- `label-caps` must always be styled with `text-transform: uppercase` and `letter-spacing: 0.08em` for micro-labels, technical badges, and state flags.

## Layout & Spacing
A 4px baseline rhythm is strictly enforced to maintain absolute precision across technical panels and compact mobile data grids.

### Mobile-First Site Grid
- **Screen Margins:** Standard `1rem` (16px) margin on mobile handsets to maximize usable horizontal drawing and table canvas; expanding to `1.5rem` on tablet/foldable site inspection modes.
- **Touch Target Integrity:** All interactive elements (inputs, toggles, selectors, action bars) must maintain a minimum physical bounding box of `48px` (ideally `56px` for primary triggers) to accommodate gloved operation on roof decks and scaffoldings.
- **Dense Data Readouts:** Nested sub-assemblies (e.g., rafter spacing, gutter pitch, flashings) utilize `0.5rem` interior padding with `1px` high-definition structural borders to prevent vertical layout bloat.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids soft, decorative drop shadows. High ambient light outside washes out subtle drop shadows, rendering them useless. Instead, depth and hierarchy are constructed through **tonal stratification, hard structural borders, and illuminated state outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Site Ground Canvas):** `#0B0F17`. Baseline bedrock.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules & Plan Cards):** `#131A26` background, bound by a crisp `1px` solid border in `#334155`.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Focus Drawers):** `#1E2738` background, bound by `1px` solid `#475569`. Used for material selectors and live measurement readouts.
- **Level 3 (AI Diagnostic & Alert Focus):** `#1E2738` surface, bound by a direct `1.5px` active accent outline (`#06B6D4` for AI active inspection, `#EF4444` for structural risk warnings, `#F59E0B` for uncommitted edits).
- **Tactile Edge Highlights:** Interactive buttons feature a subtle 1px inner top border (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) to create a solid, physical presence without relying on dark blur shadows.

## Shapes
Geometry is utilitarian, solid, and disciplined. Soft, organic shapes conflict with the linear reality of sheet metal, rafters, tiling battens, and structural framing.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard for input fields, data chips, micro-badges, and tabular calculation cells.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Structural cards, inspection preview viewports, photo overlay brackets, and modal bottom sheets.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted entirely to contextual status pills (e.g., KSeF Validated, Slope Alert, Under Review) where rounded geometry acts as an instant semantic contrast to the boxy grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Trigger:** Solid safety amber (`#F59E0B`) fill with pitch charcoal (`#0B0F17`) heavy-weight type. Minimum height `52px` on mobile. Active state shifts to `#D97706` with an inner pressed glow.
- **AI Action Trigger:** Crisp dark slate fill (`#1E2738`) with electric cyan (`#06B6D4`) border (`1.5px`) and cyan typography. Features an embedded AI diagnostic pulse icon.
- **Destructive / Structural Alert:** Dark slate fill with alert crimson (`#EF4444`) border and crimson typography.
- **Ghost Utility Button:** Transparent fill, `#94A3B8` icon/text, no border until focused (`#334155`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Compact, high-visibility capsules (`font-size: 11px`, `JetBrains Mono` uppercase).
- **Peppol / KSeF Verified:** `#10B981` background at 15% opacity, `1px` solid `#10B981`, white-green text. Includes a checkmark shield icon.
- **AI Vision Parsing:** `#06B6D4` background at 15% opacity, `1px` solid `#06B6D4`, electric cyan text. Displays live processing state with micro-ticker.
- **Defect Alert:** `#EF4444` background at 20% opacity, `1px` solid `#EF4444`, bright crimson text.

### Form Inputs & Measurement Fields
- Background `#131A26`, border `1px` solid `#334155`, minimum height `50px`.
- Numerical entries feature dedicated right-aligned unit badges (`mm`, `cm`, `m²`, `deg`, `EUR`, `PLN`) rendered in `JetBrains Mono` over a muted `#1E2738` sub-block.
- Focus state switches border to `2px` solid `#F59E0B` (Amber) or `#06B6D4` (AI auto-filled entries).

### Structural Calculation Cards
- Encased in `#131A26` with a `1px` solid `#1E2738` perimeter.
- Top card header houses a technical meta-bar with item code, surface ID, and verification status.
- Card interiors split into distinct metric blocks separating estimated surface area vs. actual ordered material (factoring in 12% scrap allowance).

### Checkboxes & Radio Toggles
- Square, industrial-spec targets (`24px x 24px` physical, with an invisible `48px` touch boundary).
- Unchecked: `#131A26` with `2px` `#475569` border.
- Checked: Solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#0B0F17` heavy glyph.

### AI Plan Viewport & Inspection Overlays
- Semi-transparent HUD overlay elements floating over the roof blueprint or drone camera view.
- Diagnostic crosshairs and facet boundaries rendered in vector `#06B6D4` (1.5px line weight) with live rafter pitch flags floating adjacent to edge vectors.
